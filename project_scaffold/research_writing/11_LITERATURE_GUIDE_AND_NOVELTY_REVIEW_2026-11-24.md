## 3. Review: novelty, what we established, what is unique

### 3.1 What is NOT new (do not claim)
- Class-conditional and Mondrian calibration give cell-wise coverage by construction (Romano 2020, Vovk 2013, Lu 2022, Ding 2023).
- Class-conditional coverage = sensitivity/specificity of two risk thresholds, so Mondrian by age = equalised-odds post-processing with unequal base rates producing unequal predictive values (Hardt 2016, Chouldechova 2017, Kleinberg 2017). We verified numerically that the two rules give identical sets for 99.2% (MIMIC) / 99.7% (eICU) of patients; the identity is not ours.
- Pooled thresholds distort group coverage, with theory (Gao 2026a) and audits (Zhong 2026).
- Marginal sets fail for the rare class (Yu 2026, Opoku 2026, Ding 2023; ours 23.5%).
- Conformal transport between MIMIC-IV and eICU (Yu 2026, Yang 2024, You 2026).
- Mondrian raises deferral/set-size disparity while lowering coverage disparity (Tasar 2025; Cresswell 2025; Gao 2026a).
Not to be claimed: "first", "novel framework / theorem / mechanism", "fair", "equitable", "provably repairs", causal effect of age.

### 3.2 What we established (all numbers from committed notes; MIMIC-IV 51,838 stays, eICU-CRD 95,558 stays, 100 seeds unless noted)
1. Pattern: with pooled class-conditional thresholds the coverage gap is two-sided and age-ordered: ages 18–49 deaths 0.840, ages 75+ survivors 0.830 (MIMIC). In eICU the stratum-minus-overall contrasts (post hoc, hospital-cluster CI) are −1.9 pp [−3.4, −0.5] (primary) / −11.0 pp (harmonised) for young deaths and −8.6 / −14.0 pp for elderly survivors; the young-decedent effect is small and model-dependent in the primary model.
2. Robustness: persists under a temporal split, logistic regression (larger gap, 0.102 vs 0.065), alpha 0.05–0.20, hospice/palliative recoding, and in 10-year age bands; absent under permuted age labels (0.026 / 0.009 vs 0.065 / 0.071); removed by dropping age (0.028) but not by dropping a random predictor.
3. Mechanism (label-free test): if scores are calibrated, pooled-threshold coverage equals a score-only quantity. It predicts the survivor-side gap with R² 0.95 (MIMIC), 0.99 (eICU, recalibrated scores) and 0.92–0.93 across 103 hospitals, and the direction and ordering of the death-side gap (magnitudes only partly; MIMIC RMSE at noise floor); it fails with raw eICU scores, exactly where calibration fails (CITL +0.042). Synthetic data show a 17 pp gap from base-rate shift at equal AUROC, none for equal prevalence with unequal AUROC, and failure under group miscalibration.
4. Repair cost: age Mondrian equalises the missed-death share (about 10% in every stratum vs 16% young / 7% old) but not predictive values (P(death | {survive}-only) 0.9% young vs 3.2% old; {die}-only precision 32% vs 47%); the age gap in set size (about 0.2 labels) is not reduced; risk-bin calibration equalises set size only by making about 80% of sets uninformative; Mondrian does not hold for young deaths under temporal shift (0.952).
5. Transport vs heterogeneity: pure transport over-covers deaths (0.920) and under-covers survivors (0.835) because eICU risk is over-predicted; eICU recalibration fixes the overall level but not the age pattern; between-hospital SD 0.045 vs 0.039 expected.
6. Small strata: hospital-specific thresholds never beat pooled; break-even deaths about 0.09/delta² (about 90–110 at shortfall 0.03).

### 3.3 What is unique (defensible wording, as far as this search shows)
No paper found combines, in ICU mortality, (i) a subgroup class-conditional coverage audit by age/race/sex/hospital, (ii) replication in a second database with transport separated from within-database heterogeneity, (iii) a label-free calibration-implied coverage test of the mechanism that fails where calibration fails, (iv) placebo and age-blind ablations, and (v) the repair-cost accounting (P(death | set), set-size equity, equalised-odds equivalence). The unique system is an audit protocol, not a new algorithm: predict group coverage from each group's score distribution, treat the residual as a miscalibration flag, test the lever by ablation against placebo, then cost the repair in predictive values and set size. The label-free test (iii) is the most distinctive element but sits next to the theory of Gao 2026a and the mechanisms of Zhong 2026; their full texts must be read before it is claimed as new.
